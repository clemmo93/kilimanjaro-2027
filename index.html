When to go

Two dry windows: January to February and June to October. No one publishes success rates by month, but rain and snow make summit night harder and have turned groups back short of the top.

    Questions to ask any operator

      Health notes at 60

      • See the GP first, with a focus on the heart. Some operators suggest a cardiac stress test over 60.
      • UK guidance says people 60 and over should not be given the yellow fever vaccine for Tanzania. A certificate is only asked for after arriving from, or spending over 12 hours in transit in, a yellow-fever country (Kenya and Ethiopia count). Flying via Amsterdam, Doha or Istanbul avoids it.
      • Malaria tablets are needed for Moshi and Arusha. Ask the GP about acetazolamide (Diamox) for altitude.
      • Insurance must cover trekking to 6,000 m and helicopter rescue. Campbell Irvine, SportsCover Direct and True Traveller all cover him at 60.
